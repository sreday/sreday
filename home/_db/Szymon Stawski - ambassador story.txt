Story (Note column):

I came to LLMday Warsaw in February as an attendee, and the very next day I wrote to Mark with one subject line: "SRE Day Warsaw - let's make it work." I wanted SREday in Warsaw, at Google. It took months of internal process, with Salim Virji helping from New York, but on October 22 we opened the doors of Google for Startups Campus to over a hundred engineers. I brought in speakers from Google and Waymo, co-hosted a hands-on workshop with Riccardo Carlesso, and walked the crew through the venue the day before. Anna, Petras and Emilia did great. It was smooth and we finally made it happen. Now I want to keep the Warsaw SRE community growing and help SREday find more homes like this one.
