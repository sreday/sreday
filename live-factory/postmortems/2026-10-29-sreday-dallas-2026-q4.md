# Postmortem: SREday Dallas 2026 Q4 in the LIVE talk factory (2026-10-29)

Blameless. What happened, why, and what changes. Written by Claude with Marek, the same night.

## Summary

One Zoom recording of the whole day (8:43 h) went through the factory on a Windows PC with an RTX 5090 ("Elephant"),
the first Windows run. 14 talks were cut and edited in Descript and are waiting for review. Two sessions are lost:
Aaron Hunter's panel (room mic muted in Zoom) and the Sanjay / Girish Kotte panel (the recording stopped 5 min in).
Parts of three more talks are silent. The factory stopped three times on the way: one Whisper crash and two dead runs.
The two dead runs cost about 30 min of waiting and restarting.

## What went well

- CUDA Whisper on the 5090: the whole day transcribed in ~15 min; NVENC cuts.
- Credit guard 2 worked: after each restart, edits Descript had already finished were skipped, not paid twice.
- Frames every 120 s plus the level line settled every doubtful match (the "sounds like" false alarm, Michael's blank
  picture).
- Marek's answers came in one round because the questions came together with the evidence.

## What went wrong, and why

1. **Lost content at the venue** (biggest loss: 1 panel, 3 openings, 1 talk's end, plus the closing panel).
   - Why: the room mic was muted in Zoom at times, and nobody saw it, because nothing on the stage shows the level of the recording.
   - Why: the recording was stopped before the end of the day.
   - Why: there is no recording checklist for the venue and no backup recorder. A factory can't fix this.
2. **Run killed by a Windows file lock** (01:48).
   - Why: `os.replace` fails on Windows while another process has the target open, and the workbench reads state.json
     every few seconds.
   - Why it wasn't caught: all earlier days ran on macOS, and the smoke test runs one process.
   - The same pattern was also in reviews.json, redo.json, cuts.json, transcripts and status.json.
     reviews.json would have hit at gate 2, when Marek ticks Reviewed during a run.
3. **Run and workbench died together** (03:11). Root cause not found. Leading suspect: both belonged to a shell session
   of Claude Code that ended, which would take its process tree down with it. This is not tested. Nobody noticed for a while,
   because the workbench that would show a dead run was gone too.
4. **Unpinned dependency broke transcription.** faster-whisper and PyAV float. A new PyAV release broke the pair overnight.
5. **Lineup gap.** The host's own talk (Jeremy, 12:00) wasn't in talks.csv, so the matcher had no key for it.
6. **Setup on a new Windows PC took manual steps.** `setup.cmd` didn't start from Claude's shells, and `python` wasn't on PATH.

## Actions

Done now (pushed with this postmortem):

- `machine.swap_in` (retry up to 5 s) now does every JSON swap that the workbench and a run share: state, reviews, redo,
  cuts, transcripts and status. Tested: a plain `os.replace` against an open file fails with "Access is denied", and
  `swap_in` succeeds once the reader lets go.
- `av<16` pinned.
- New rules in the live-factory skill:
  - Cut every closing Q&A and leave the margin.
  - Ask about talks that aren't in the lineup, then add them as speakers and hand over the WWW row.
  - Explain muted Zoom stretches and ask for another source.
  - Send doubtful talks to Descript when told to.
  - Ignore a `draft` that refuses to save.
  - Start long jobs with Start-Process, and restart both processes after a silent stop.
- README, MANUAL and MANUAL-CLAUDE updated.
