## Summary

11 files from the event drive: 3 track cameras (r1, r6, r9), 1 phone clip (r2, not used) and 7 WAV recorders laid in
as the sound. 28 talks in the lineup, 27 shipped to the SREday channel, 1 no-show (Yitian Xu, confirmed by the MC on
the recording). The event is more than 5 weeks past, so the videos went public at once.

Every talk's start and end were set by hand in cuts.json ("ends"), because the automatic edges were often wrong. That
exposed two factory bugs: the check after the cut compared a hand-set edge with the words of the old automatic edge
(20 talks stopped before Descript), and adding words to an edge counted as a changed cut (4 talks already on YouTube
were reset). Both were fixed in the night; nothing was uploaded twice and no Descript credits were wasted on them.

Descript's transcript failed on two talks. Its edit cut real speech: Michael Cade lost 53% (stopped by the selfcheck),
and Gabriel Okiri lost his 7 s introduction (shipped on the benefit of the doubt, found by listening afterwards). Both
were redone with a new sound-only edit.

## What went well

- The lineup came out right on all three tracks, including a no-show, a talk on the "wrong" track (Ozlem on track
  2) and a speaker whose laptop died mid-talk (Rob Reid, kept).
- The separate recorder sound was in sync on every talk (worst offset 11 ms); no clipped sound anywhere.
- The check before Descript did its job: every stop was before a credit was spent, and Claude's fixes went through
  cuts.json without asking anyone at night.
- The 07:44 mistake was caught by reading the run's first log lines; stopping at once kept YouTube and Descript clean.
- The selfcheck stopped Michael Cade's half-cut talk from going to YouTube.

## What went wrong, and why

1. **20 talks stopped on a check that compared the wrong words.** The factory had only ever seen hand-set edges near
   the automatic ones. Here 23 of 27 edges moved, some by a minute. The found sentence belongs to the found edge.
2. **4 finished talks were reset by a cuts.json edit that changed nothing in the cut.** The signature over the whole
   "ends" dict treated a note ("why", "first", "last") as a cut change. Claude also added words before checking what a
   cuts.json change does to talks already done.
3. **Descript's transcript dropped speech on 2 talks, and the gap/filler edit cut it.** The selfcheck only catches it
   when the loss is large (Michael 53%); Gabriel's 7 s at the start shipped with an "edges" doubt. Middle-of-talk
   losses are not checked at all.
4. **The trim to subtitles trusts Descript's cues.** When the transcript is wrong, the trim cuts words too.
5. **The selfcheck's edge doubts ship anyway.** "The cut starts on X, not on Y" on a final is a missing opening more
   often than a Whisper mishearing; Gabriel's was real.
